Prime Minister Section Costa was ambassador to Portugal from 2000 to 2002. He was appointed
as Prime Minister to lead a coalition government in April 2002. However, he was sacked from
that post on September 27, 2002 by President Fredique de Menzies after army unrest over
two controversial promotions. On December 3, 2012, he was again appointed as Prime Minister

his parliamentary majority.
