Prime Minister Section
Costa was Ambassador to Portugal from 2000 to 2002. He was appointed as Prime Minister
to lead a coalition government in April 2002. However, he was sacked from that post on September
27, 2002 by President Fredike de Menzies after army unrest over two controversial promotions.
On December 3, 2012, he was again appointed as Prime Minister by President Manuel Pinto
da Costa following the dismissal of Patrice Trovada, who had lost his parliamentary majority
